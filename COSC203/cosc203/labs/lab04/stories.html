Polly the Penguin's Stories 🐧

Stories about my life as a penguin!

Wrong Way Round

I finally booked my dream trip to Antarctica, only to discover everyone expected me to already live there.

I smiled, waved my flippers, and declared that every great adventure starts with confusing the travel agent.

The Souvenir Problem

I promised to bring back a unique souvenir from Antarctica for my friends.

After three days, I realized everyone was selling snow, and it all looked suspiciously familiar.

First Impressions

I stepped onto the ice expecting dramatic music to begin playing.

Instead, another penguin asked if I could move because I was standing in the queue for fish.

The Tourist Map

I carried a detailed map of Antarctica everywhere I went.

Unfortunately, every direction simply said, "More ice this way."

Packing Mistakes

I packed sunglasses, a woolly hat, and a tiny umbrella for my Antarctic adventure.

The sunglasses were useful. The umbrella became the fanciest sled on the continent.

Family Reunion

I arrived in Antarctica hoping to meet my distant relatives.

Within an hour, everyone insisted we looked exactly alike, which made introductions surprisingly difficult.

The Iceberg Cruise

I signed up for an iceberg sightseeing tour to experience the local scenery.

The guide proudly pointed at a slightly larger iceberg and called it "the deluxe edition."

Finding My Voice

I practiced my most impressive penguin call before arriving in Antarctica.

The locals applauded politely, then informed me I had been accidentally ordering extra fish.

The Scenic Shortcut

I tried taking a shortcut across the ice to save five minutes.

After an hour of sliding in circles, I arrived exactly where I had started, but much more stylishly.

Did a backflip!

Today I was trying to slide down a slope to dive into the water, ended up doing a pretty sick backflip!

Too bad no one was around to see it, ah well. Got some pretty nice fish though.
